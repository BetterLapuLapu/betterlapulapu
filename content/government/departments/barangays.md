# Barangays of Lapu-Lapu City

Lapu-Lapu City has 30 barangays, each led by an elected punong barangay (barangay captain), seven barangay kagawads (councilors) and a Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) chairperson. This page lists the punong barangay and SK chairperson of every barangay for the 2023–2026 term.

## What Barangay Officials Do

The **punong barangay** is the chief executive of the barangay. They enforce barangay ordinances, issue barangay clearances and certifications, and head the Lupong Tagapamayapa, which settles disputes between residents before they go to court.

The **Sangguniang Barangay** (barangay council) passes barangay ordinances and approves the barangay budget. Its members are the punong barangay, who presides, the seven elected kagawads and the SK chairperson.

The **SK chairperson** leads the Sangguniang Kabataan, which runs the barangay's youth programs, and sits on the barangay council as an ex officio member.

The **barangay secretary** and **barangay treasurer** are appointed by the punong barangay with the approval of the barangay council.

The punong barangays elect a president of the city chapter of the **Liga ng mga Barangay**, and the SK chairpersons elect a **SK Federation president**. Both sit on the City Council as ex officio members. To learn about the rest of the council, see the [Legislative Department](/government/departments/legislative) page.
